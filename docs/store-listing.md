## Full description (4000 max)
Can you build wealth, raise a family and retire comfortably?

FinLit is a pixel-art life simulator that teaches financial literacy by letting you make real money decisions with zero real-world risk. Start at 18 with a small balance, pick your character, and play month by month through jobs, investments, family and the surprises life throws at you.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Build a career: apply for jobs, earn raises, study for degrees and handle sick leave
• Invest: trade stocks, buy mutual funds, run a monthly SIP, and park money in fixed deposits, PPF, NPS and gold
• Own property: rent or buy homes and commercial property, and watch maintenance, loans and values change
• Manage debt: take loans, pay EMIs, use credit cards wisely and protect your credit score
• Stay protected: buy insurance before a crisis hits, not after
• Pay your taxes: file your income tax return, choose between doing it yourself or hiring a CA, and avoid late fees
• Raise a family: marry, have children, look after dependents and celebrate birthdays, while keeping everyone healthy and happy
• Survive the unexpected: market crashes, medical bills, job changes and family demands test your savings
• Plan for retirement and see what you leave behind. Your children can carry your story into the next generation

LEARN BY PLAYING
Every choice has a consequence. Spend freely and you may struggle later. Save and invest wisely and your money grows. The game is set in India and uses rupees, with the products and rules real people deal with, such as SIPs, PPF, NPS, ITR filing and EMIs.

SAFE AND SIMPLE
• All money in the game is virtual
• No ads
• No account or sign-up needed
• Your progress is saved on your device
• Works offline

Whether you're a student, a young earner, a parent or just curious about money, FinLit makes it fun to learn how it works.

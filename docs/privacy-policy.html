FinLit Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

FinLit ("the app", "we") is an educational financial literacy game. This policy explains what information the app handles. In short: we do not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information.

Information we collect

None. The app has no accounts, no sign-up, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no third-party tracking tools.

Information stored on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the app saves your game progress on your device only. This includes the character name you type in, your chosen character, your birthday setting inside the game, and your in-game money, assets and choices. This data never leaves your device. All money in FinLit is virtual and has no real-world value. You can delete it at any time by starting a new game in the app, clearing the app's storage in your device settings, or uninstalling the app.

Permissions

The app does not request any special device permissions. It does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, files or location.

Network use and third parties

The game is designed to be played offline. It does not send your game data or any personal information to us or to any third party, and it contains no third-party advertising or analytics code.

Children's privacy

FinLit is suitable for young players precisely because it collects no personal information from anyone, including children. Because we collect nothing, there is no child data to share, sell or delete from our side.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app adds features that handle data, such as ads or in-app purchases, we will update this policy and the information shown on the Google Play listing before that version is released. The date above shows when this policy was last changed.
